This fingerstyle guitar arrangement of Tell Me by Joey Albert captures the emotional depth of one of OPM's most iconic love songs. Joey Albert's powerful melody is carried on solo acoustic guitar, with fingerpicked harmonies supporting every phrase.

Released in the 1980s, Tell Me remains a staple of Filipino love song playlists and one of Joey Albert's most requested performances. Playing it fingerstyle means balancing three parts at once — the singing melody, the lush chords underneath, and a steady bass — so the arrangement keeps all the longing of the original without a single word.

A timeless OPM ballad reimagined for solo fingerstyle guitar.
